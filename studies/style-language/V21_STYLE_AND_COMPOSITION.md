## Correct next

Derive its pitches from the same cell, use longer-spanning reductions, and avoid separate two/three-note pendulums. Maintain interior motion beneath it. Stable tuning remains essential: no random pitch wobble to simulate dirt. Softness must be judged in the rendered sound, not inferred from attack values.
