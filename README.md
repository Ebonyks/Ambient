# Ambient — music production research

## Harmony protocol and construction tools

[Read the synthesis and allowed/conditional/excluded harmony rules](protocols/hecker-harmony-v1/README.md).

The protocol adds 15 transposable source families, four construction profiles, interval-pattern statistics, a deterministic generator/validator, and four original MIDI/JSON sketches. Its exclusions are scoped design rules, not claims that the artist never uses a chord.

## Tim Hecker: Radio Amor, Mirages and Mort aux Vaches

[Read the full measured production study](studies/tim-hecker/Tim_Hecker_Production_Study.md)

An audio-derived study covering all 22 Bandcamp recordings in 233 analysis windows, with harmonic interpretations, pitch frequencies and tuning offsets, reconstruction patches, and live/studio correspondences.

- [Download the complete package](studies/tim-hecker/Hecker_Measured_Study.zip)
- [Download the self-contained HTML report](studies/tim-hecker/Tim_Hecker_Production_Study.html) and open it locally for navigation and embedded charts.

- [Data formats and reproduction instructions](studies/tim-hecker/README.md)
- [Publication manifest and hashes](DELIVERY_MANIFEST.json)

Measured spectral components, inferred harmonies and proposed settings are distinguished. This is not a verified score, a recovered original session, or a trained model. Source recordings are not redistributed.
